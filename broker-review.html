Overview

XM Group is one of the largest and most established retail forex brokers globally, serving more than 5 million clients from over 190 countries. Operating since 2009, the group is regulated by multiple tier-one financial authorities including CySEC, ASIC, FCA and IFSC, providing clients with the assurance of segregated client funds and participation in investor compensation schemes.

XM's offering is centered on accessibility and education — the broker is particularly known for its low minimum deposit threshold, generous bonus program and one of the most comprehensive free educational libraries in the retail FX industry. Whether you're a complete beginner testing with a $5 micro lot or an experienced trader running algorithmic strategies, XM's infrastructure supports a broad audience.

Pros & Cons

What we like

  • Multi-jurisdictional regulation including FCA, ASIC and CySEC
  • Industry-low $5 minimum deposit and micro lot trading
  • No fees on deposits or withdrawals across most methods
  • 24/5 multilingual customer support with sub-minute response times
  • Generous welcome bonus and loyalty program
  • Comprehensive free educational resources and live daily webinars
  • Tight spreads on major pairs from 0.6 pips on standard accounts

What we don't

  • Bonus availability restricted in certain regulated jurisdictions (EU, AU)
  • Limited product range compared to multi-asset competitors
  • Inactivity fee of $15 after 90 days for dormant accounts
  • No support for crypto-to-crypto trading pairs
  • Spreads on exotic pairs can widen materially during off-hours

Fees & Spreads

XM uses a transparent spread-based pricing model with no commission on standard accounts and a $3.50 per-side commission on the Ultra Low account. Average spreads on EUR/USD start from 1.0 pip on Standard and 0.6 pips on Ultra Low — competitive with the broader industry though not the absolute tightest.

There are no fees on deposits or withdrawals via most payment methods, including bank wire, credit/debit card and major e-wallets. An inactivity fee of $15 per quarter applies after 90 days of dormancy, which is standard but worth noting for casual users.

Trading Platforms

XM supports the two industry-standard MetaQuotes platforms, MT4 and MT5, across desktop, web and mobile. Both are available in their full-featured versions with support for Expert Advisors, custom indicators and algorithmic trading. XM does not currently offer a proprietary platform, though its MT5 integration includes some custom add-ons and market depth improvements.

MT4 — Best for EAs

The industry standard for algorithmic trading. Supports 30+ preinstalled indicators, 9 timeframes and MQL4 scripting. Ideal for traders running existing EA libraries.

MT5 — Best for multi-asset

Newer generation platform with native support for stocks, commodities and indices. Includes depth of market, advanced order types and the integrated MQL5 development suite.

Regulation & Safety

XM operates through multiple regulated entities across the world, each providing segregated client funds and protection according to local rules:

  • XM Global (CySEC, Cyprus) — License 120/10, serving EEA clients under MiFID II
  • XM Australia (ASIC) — AFSL 443670, serving Australian clients
  • XM UK (FCA) — Reference 705428, serving UK clients under FSCS protection
  • XM International (IFSC) — Serving international clients outside the above jurisdictions

Client funds are held in segregated accounts at tier-one banks, separate from the firm's operating capital. Negative balance protection is provided across all retail accounts.

Account Types

XM offers four main retail account types plus an Islamic swap-free variant for each. The Micro account is particularly accessible at a $5 minimum deposit and micro lot sizing, while the Ultra Low account delivers the tightest spreads for high-volume traders. There's no VIP or premium tier — instead, XM provides enhanced services through its loyalty points program available to all account holders.

Deposits & Withdrawals

XM supports over 10 deposit methods with no fees on its side. Most deposits are processed instantly, while withdrawals typically complete within 24 hours. Available methods include bank wire, Visa, Mastercard, Skrill, Neteller, WebMoney, UnionPay and several regional payment systems. The minimum withdrawal amount is generally $5 for cards and e-wallets.

Customer Support

XM's customer support is widely regarded as one of the best in the retail FX industry. The broker offers 24/5 live chat, email and phone support in over 30 languages. Our test queries received responses in under 45 seconds during market hours, with knowledgeable agents capable of resolving technical, account and platform issues. There's also a comprehensive self-service knowledge base and active community forums.

Final Verdict

XM Group remains one of the most accessible and well-regulated retail forex brokers in the industry. Its combination of low entry requirements, multi-jurisdictional regulation, transparent pricing and exceptional customer support make it a strong choice for traders at every experience level — though serious scalpers and algorithmic traders may prefer brokers with ECN-style raw spreads.
